# Energy Upgrade Solutions Marketing Website

## Goal

Create a polished, responsive React marketing website for Energy Upgrade Solutions, based on the topics and factual details on the supplied reference website. Use original, paraphrased copy; do not reproduce the reference's wording verbatim.

## Approved scope

- Single-page public marketing website.
- React with JavaScript (not TypeScript), as confirmed by the user.
- No backend, database, authentication, or persistent visitor data.
- Responsive layouts and accessible semantic markup.
- Clear service information and enquiry/eligibility calls to action.

## Content

Cover the Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) program and explain the upgrade journey: eligibility check, home assessment, and installation. Include service sections for heat-pump hot water, heating and cooling, and solar PV and batteries; a concise rebate overview; customer-trust/testimonial content; and contact/eligibility calls to action.

The reference describes incentives of up to A$7,000 for eligible upgrades, solar panel rebates and interest-free loans of up to A$1,400 each, and eligible battery rebates of up to A$8,800. If these amounts are included, label them as conditional, subject to eligibility and current program rules, and do not imply guaranteed savings or approval. Do not invent customer testimonials, contact details, credentials, or eligibility criteria; use clearly identified placeholders only when necessary.

## Design direction

Build an attractive, trustworthy clean-energy brand experience with a confident hero, warm neutral backgrounds, deep green accents, strong typography, generous spacing, clear service cards, and prominent calls to action. Use mobile-first responsive behavior, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion-friendly interactions. Use locally available or appropriately attributed visual assets; avoid depending on the reference site's branding or copying its written expression.

## Acceptance criteria

- The React app installs and runs using the repository's existing conventions (or a suitable lightweight setup if none exists).
- Visitors can understand the offering, the main upgrade categories, and that rebate eligibility is conditional.
- Navigation and calls to action lead to meaningful sections or a usable contact path; do not imply a form submits unless it is wired to a service.
- The site works on mobile and desktop, with no fabricated proof points or unsupported claims.
